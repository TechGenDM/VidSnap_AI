## Testing Protocol & Observer Guide

### 1. Objective Given to Tester
Every tester receives only this unguided prompt:
> **“Create a 30-second Reel explaining something you know.”**
> *(No explanation of presets, Ask VidSnap, b-roll upload, or timeline features)*

### 2. Observation Dimensions
* **First Action:** What is the tester's very first click and choice?
* **Mental Model:** Do they understand what a "Storyboard" is? Do they expect a video timeline?
* **Unprompted Discoverability:**
  - Did they find **Try Another Hook**?
  - Did they find **Ask VidSnap**?
  - Did they edit a scene's text directly?
  - Did they notice **Custom B-Roll** or image alternatives?
  - Did they explore **Version History** or **Duplicate**?
* **Trust & Scrutiny:**
  - Did they inspect every scene before rendering?
  - Did they listen to the full audio before or after rendering?
  - Did they distrust any AI claim or wording?
* **First Reel vs. Second Reel:**
  - Prompt for Reel #2: *"Now create another Reel on a completely different topic."*
  - Does the workflow become noticeably faster, more confident, and less hesitant?
